Conventions worth stating once

RuleWhy
Never colour aloneEvery state carries a glyph or a word. Survives greyscale, colour blindness, and bad screens.
Focus ring is always blueIt must read as "you are here" across two palettes and both themes, and stay visible on the accent fill.
Dashed border means "creates something new"One border style, one meaning, everywhere — pills, suggestions, and stale badges.
Fraunces never sets proseIt is a display face. Notes are the app's main content and get a face built for reading.
Mono for machine dataHosts, handles, counts, operators, dates. Mono marks it as data rather than prose, which is what makes dense lists scannable.
