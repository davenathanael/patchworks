Patchworks — converged design

Cards' information model with Split Sheet's interaction and visual language. Card on mobile, row past 54rem. The note is a paragraph, so the list shows an excerpt and expands in place. What to judge

The note strategy. Excerpt-and-expand is the load-bearing decision here. It keeps the list scannable while letting a paragraph be a paragraph. Check it against real note lengths — if your typical note is five lines rather than three, the two-line excerpt may be too stingy.

The share page. This is new and untested by any earlier round. It inverts the normal hierarchy: the note leads, the title supports. Worth deciding whether that inversion is right before anything is built on top of it.

The picker feel-check in section 6. Both variants are live in the same file; you can switch skins while comparing them, since the glyph approach and the corrected pills interact differently with each palette.

Unresolved and deliberately left open: whether the shared page should expose unread state. Right now a visitor sees everything, including the twelve items you haven't read. That's arguably the point of sharing a research collection, but it's a real decision, not an oversight.
