Round 3 — one design, four skins

The Ledger candidate is out. What remains from rounds 1 and 2 is a single converged design: Cards' information model (note-first, card on mobile, row on desktop) built on Split Sheet's interaction and visual language (inspection sheet, 2px corners, permanently visible row actions). This round builds that design completely and dresses it in four skins you can switch between live.

Decisions carried into round 3

Baseline
Mobile-first. Desktop shows more of the same thing, never a different design.
Item shape
Card on mobile, aligned row past ~54rem. Same data both ways.
Long notes
Excerpt of four lines in the list, expanding in place on tap. A note is a paragraph, not a caption — nothing is lost, and the list stays scannable.
Inspection
Selecting an item opens a bottom sheet on mobile with a top-edge shadow, or a docked side pane at desktop widths. The note is edited there as a real textarea.
Tags
Text #hashtags on items; small neutral pills in pickers, dashed border reserved for "create new". The glyph alternative is kept in section 6 for reference.
Collections
A display title and a separate @handle, both editable. "Databases" can be typed as @databases; "Project A" as @project-a.
Quick add
One <dialog>. Full-screen on mobile with the header inside the panel; a centred popover on desktop.
Reading
No in-app reader. The queue is a list; items open the original in a new tab. Current item pinned to the top.
Feeds
Path A, committed. Feed items are ephemeral until you act: added to the reading queue, saved as a bookmark, or dismissed.
Account
A fifth tab — Inbox, Queue, Feeds, Collections, You. Profile, settings, subscriptions, import/export and sign-out live there.
Share page
Public, read-only, one page per collection. Shows every bookmark with its tags and notes, regardless of your own reading or queue state.
Depth
Borders plus a button shadow. Floating surfaces get a real shadow. Nothing else is elevated.
Dark mode
Backgrounds sit at oklch L≈0.27 rather than the usual 0.18–0.20. Body text lands at 13:1 against the background, secondary at 8:1, muted metadata at 5.5:1 — all passing WCAG AA.
Wide screens
Cap the width and grow the type. No extra columns, no docked panels.

The two skins

Both share identical markup. The switcher at the top of the design file changes only tokens and typefaces — which is the point: you are judging the look against a structure that is already settled. Two skins × light/dark, all four combinations.
